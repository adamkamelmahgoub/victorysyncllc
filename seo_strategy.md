# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public marketing pages at the repo root app
- Service pages (`/services`, `/inbound-support`, `/appointment-setting`, `/cold-calling`, `/ai-automation`)
- Company and conversion pages (`/`, `/pricing`, `/about-us`, `/about`, `/contact`, `/platform`, `/book-call`)
- Public content and support pages (`/blog`, `/blog/:slug`, `/careers`, `/help-center`, `/docs`, `/privacy-policy`, `/terms`)

## Out of scope
- Authenticated product area (`/dashboard`)
- Auth and checkout flows (`/login`, `/register`, `/signup`, `/checkout`)
- Archived duplicate app under `VictorySync/`

## Target audience
- US businesses evaluating outsourced contact center, appointment setting, inbound support, outbound calling, and AI automation services

- inbound customer support

- AI automation for customer service
