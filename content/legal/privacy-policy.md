# Privacy Policy

**Victory Sync LLC**
**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Victory Sync LLC, doing business as VictorySync ("VictorySync," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information, and the choices and rights available to you.

---

## 1. Scope and Who This Policy Covers

1.1. This Policy applies to personal information we collect through:

- victorysync.com and its subdomains, including dashboard.victorysync.com, consult.victorysync.com, and join.victorysync.com (the "Sites");
- our client dashboard, CRM, reporting tools, and related software (the "Platform");
- our contact center, sales, digital marketing, lead generation, AI and automation, and consulting services (the "Services"); and
- our communications with you by phone, text, email, WhatsApp, video, social media, and in person.

1.2. This Policy covers the following groups of people:

- **Visitors** to our Sites;
- **Prospects and clients**, meaning business representatives who inquire about, purchase, or use our Services;
- **Call and message recipients**, meaning individuals our personnel contact, or who contact us, on behalf of our clients;
- **Business contacts** whose professional information we research for our own sales outreach;
- **Job applicants and contractors**, including people who apply through join.victorysync.com; and
- **Platform users**, including client users and our own personnel who use our dashboard.

1.3. **When we act on behalf of our clients.** When we contact people or handle data on behalf of a client (for example, calling leads that a client gave us, answering a client's inbound phone line, or managing a client's ad campaigns), we process that data as a service provider or processor for that client, following the client's instructions. The client's own privacy policy governs how the client collects and uses that data. If you are a customer or prospect of one of our clients, please contact that business directly with privacy requests. We will assist our clients in responding to those requests as required by law.

1.4. This Policy does not apply to third-party websites, platforms, or services that we link to or integrate with, which are governed by their own privacy policies.

1.5. If you have signed a separate agreement with us, such as a data processing agreement, that agreement controls where it conflicts with this Policy.

## 2. Personal Information We Collect

The categories below include information we have collected in the last twelve (12) months.

### 2.1. Information you provide to us

- **Contact and business information:** name, company, job title, business email, phone numbers, mailing address, website, and social media profiles.
- **Account information:** login credentials, user role, and preferences for the Platform.
- **Communications:** messages, emails, chat and WhatsApp messages, form submissions, meeting notes, feedback, and survey responses.
- **Engagement information:** your campaign goals, scripts, qualification criteria, offers, target audiences, and other information you provide to set up Services.
- **Billing information:** billing contact details, payment history, and limited payment details processed by our payment processors. We do not store full payment card numbers.
- **Job application information:** name, contact details, location, resume or CV, work history, education, language skills, voice or audio samples, availability, references, and any other information you choose to provide in an application or interview.

### 2.2. Information we collect while providing the Services

- **Call and message data:** call recordings, voicemails, transcripts, call summaries, call logs, phone numbers, dates, times, durations, and outcomes, where permitted by law.
- **Lead and customer data** provided by or collected for our clients: names, contact details, property, business, or service interest information, appointment details, notes, dispositions, and any other information a client or a call recipient shares.
- **Do not call and opt-out records:** records of requests not to be contacted, so that we can honor them.
- **Advertising and marketing data:** campaign performance data, audience and targeting data, lead form submissions, and analytics from advertising and social media platforms we manage for clients.

### 2.3. Information we collect automatically

- **Device and usage information:** IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages viewed, links clicked, referring URLs, time spent, and approximate location derived from IP address.
- **Cookies, pixels, and similar technologies:** see Section 6.

### 2.4. Information from other sources

- **Business contact data** from publicly available sources, business directories, websites, review and map platforms, and commercial data providers, which we use to research and enrich business leads.
- **Information from our clients** about their leads and customers.
- **Information from marketplaces** such as Fiverr and Upwork when you engage us through them.
- **Information from advertising and social media platforms**, including lead forms and analytics.
- **Information from referral partners and references** you provide.

### 2.5. Personnel monitoring data

For our own agents and contractors who use our Platform, we collect work activity data, which may include log-in and log-out times, hours worked, call activity, keyboard and mouse activity counts, periodic screenshots, and quality assurance evaluations. This data is used to manage work, verify time, ensure quality and security, and provide clients with view-only reports about agents assigned to their accounts. Personnel are notified of this monitoring as part of their engagement.

### 2.6. Sensitive information

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, health information, or precise geolocation through our Sites or general Services, and we ask clients not to send it to us unless a specific written agreement covers it. If you are a job applicant, we may collect limited information needed to verify identity or eligibility to work, and only as permitted by law.

## 3. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information to:

- (a) provide, operate, maintain, and deliver the Services and Platform, including making and receiving calls, qualifying leads, booking appointments, transferring calls, managing advertising campaigns, and producing reports;
- (b) set up and manage client accounts, onboarding, billing, payments, and collections;
- (c) communicate with you, including responding to inquiries, scheduling consultations, sending service updates, confirmations, reminders, invoices, and administrative notices;
- (d) monitor and record calls for quality assurance, training, coaching, compliance, and dispute resolution;
- (e) honor do not call and opt-out requests;
- (f) market our own services to businesses, including through email, phone, social media, and advertising, subject to your choices;
- (g) research, identify, and enrich business leads for our own sales efforts and, as directed, for our clients;
- (h) use AI and automation tools to transcribe, summarize, analyze, score, route, and enrich information, as described in Section 5;
- (i) recruit, evaluate, hire, onboard, and manage personnel;
- (j) analyze and improve our Sites, Platform, and Services, and develop new features;
- (k) protect the security and integrity of our systems, prevent fraud and abuse, and verify identity;
- (l) comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our Terms of Service and agreements; and
- (m) carry out any other purpose disclosed to you at the time of collection or with your consent.

We may create aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you and use it for any lawful purpose. We will not attempt to re-identify de-identified information except as permitted by law.

## 4. How We Disclose Personal Information

We disclose personal information to the following categories of recipients:

- **Our clients:** when we provide Services on a client's behalf, we share call outcomes, recordings, notes, appointment details, and lead information with that client. Clients may also view activity reports about agents assigned to their account.
- **Service providers and subprocessors:** companies that help us operate, under contracts that limit their use of personal information, including providers of website hosting, cloud databases, telephony and dialing, CRM, email delivery, text messaging, scheduling, workflow automation, AI and transcription, data enrichment, analytics, payments, accounting, customer support, and communication tools.
- **Advertising and social media platforms:** when we run campaigns for ourselves or our clients, and through pixels and similar technologies described in Section 6.
- **Marketplaces:** platforms such as Fiverr and Upwork, when you engage us through them.
- **Professional advisors:** lawyers, accountants, auditors, and insurers.
- **Legal and safety recipients:** courts, regulators, law enforcement, and other parties when we believe disclosure is required by law, legal process, or a government request, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of VictorySync, our clients, our personnel, or others, or to enforce our agreements.
- **Business transfers:** a buyer, successor, or other party in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of our business or assets.
- **With your consent or direction:** any other party you authorize.

We do not sell personal information for money. See Section 11 for information about advertising-related "sharing" under certain U.S. state laws.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging opt-in data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

5.1. We use AI and automation tools, including tools provided by third parties, to support our personnel and Services. ## 6. Cookies, Pixels, and Similar Technologies

6.1. We and our partners use cookies, pixels, tags, local storage, and similar technologies to operate the Sites, remember preferences, keep you signed in, understand how visitors use our Sites, measure the effectiveness of our advertising, and show you relevant advertising on other websites and platforms.

6.2. The types of technologies we use include:

- **Strictly necessary:** required for the Sites and Platform to function, such as authentication and security. These cannot be turned off.
- **Analytics:** help us understand traffic and usage.
- **Advertising and marketing:** including the Meta Pixel and similar tools, which allow advertising platforms to collect information about your visits to our Sites and use it to measure and target advertising.

6.3. **Your choices.** You can manage cookies through our cookie settings tool where available, through your browser settings, and through the advertising preference settings of platforms like Meta and Google. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Sites work.

6.4. **Global Privacy Control.** We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising, as required by applicable law. Because there is no common industry standard for "Do Not Track" signals, we do not respond to them.

## 7. Communications Choices

7.1. **Marketing emails.** You can opt out of our marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or contacting us. We may still send you transactional and service messages.

7.2. **Calls.** If you do not want to receive calls from us, tell the person calling you or contact us at info@victorysync.com. We will add your number to our internal do not call list. If we called you on behalf of a client, we will also notify that client of your request.

7.3. **SMS Program.** If you opt in to our service-related SMS Program, we process your mobile number and message content to send confirmations, reminders, and status updates. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

**Opt-out:** reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT.
**Help:** reply HELP or email info@victorysync.com.

Texts are sent through registered A2P 10DLC and verified toll-free programs using vetted providers and are intended for non-marketing communications unless you separately and expressly consent to marketing messages. We do not use public URL shorteners in text messages. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Our personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations and access data only as needed to perform their work.

## 9. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to provide the Services, comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods depend on the type of data and our relationship with you. As a general guide:

- **Client account and billing records:** for the duration of the relationship and up to seven (7) years afterward for tax and legal purposes.
- **Call recordings and transcripts:** as instructed by the client, or otherwise up to 12 months from the date of the call, unless needed longer for a dispute or legal hold.
- **Lead and campaign data handled for clients:** for the duration of the engagement and up to thirty (30) days after it ends, unless the client instructs otherwise or a longer period is required by law.
- **Do not call and opt-out records:** for at least five (5) years, so that we can continue to honor them.
- **Job applicant information:** up to 12 months after the application, so we can consider you for future roles, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or law requires otherwise.
- **Website analytics data:** according to the retention settings of our analytics providers.

When we no longer need personal information, we delete it, de-identify it, or securely store it until deletion is possible.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including access controls, role-based permissions, encryption in transit, and confidentiality obligations for personnel. Access to client data is limited to personnel and systems that need it for the approved workflow. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your Platform credentials confidential. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us immediately at info@victorysync.com. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the appropriate authorities as required by law.

## 11. Your U.S. State Privacy Rights

11.1. Depending on where you live, including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Virginia, and other states with consumer privacy laws, you may have some or all of the following rights, subject to legal exceptions:

- **Right to know and access:** to confirm whether we process your personal information and to access the categories and specific pieces of personal information we hold about you.
- **Right to correct:** to correct inaccurate personal information.
- **Right to delete:** to request deletion of personal information we collected from you.
- **Right to portability:** to receive a copy of your personal information in a portable format.
- **Right to opt out:** of the sale of personal information, the sharing or processing of personal information for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising, and profiling in furtherance of decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
- **Right to limit** the use of sensitive personal information, where applicable.
- **Right to non-discrimination:** we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

11.2. **Advertising "sharing."** We do not sell personal information for money. However, our use of advertising pixels and similar technologies on our Sites may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws. You can opt out by using the "Your Privacy Choices" link on our Sites, by enabling Global Privacy Control in your browser, or by contacting us.

11.3. **Categories (California).** In the last twelve (12) months, we have collected the following categories of personal information described in the California Consumer Privacy Act: identifiers; customer records information; commercial information; internet or other electronic network activity information; approximate geolocation; audio and electronic information (such as call recordings); professional or employment-related information; and inferences drawn from the above. We collect these categories from the sources in Section 2, use them for the purposes in Section 3, and disclose them to the categories of recipients in Section 4. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under sixteen (16).

11.4. **How to exercise your rights.** Email privacy@victorysync.com or info@victorysync.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will need to verify your identity before responding, which may require you to confirm information we already have. You may use an authorized agent, who must provide proof of authorization, and we may still ask you to verify your identity directly. We will respond within the time required by law.

11.5. **Appeals.** If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject line "Privacy Appeal." We will respond to your appeal within the time required by law. If you are not satisfied, you may contact your state attorney general.

11.6. **Requests about data we process for clients.** If we processed your information on behalf of a client, we may refer your request to that client, who is responsible for responding.

11.7. **Employee and applicant information.** Where state law grants rights to job applicants and personnel, those individuals may exercise them using the same process.
